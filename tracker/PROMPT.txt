   DISCIPLINE RULES (they override anything in the option chain; the record before 27 Nov was a coin flip because these were missing)
   a. TREND FIRST. Lean only in the direction trend.lean_allowed gives ("up", "down" or "none"). "none" means no lean: a range call or wait. An intraday run KEEPS the open call, its range, lean, zones and stop, word for word in substance, unless its invalidation has been hit; it only refreshes the numbers around it. A new hour of data, a new wall or a PCR change is not a reason to change the call. If the invalidation is hit: mark the call wrong, and open ONE replacement only if calls_opened_today is 0 (or there is no open call at the first run of the day). If a call opened today has already been stopped, open nothing: light = "red", trade = null, headline says the plan for today was stopped and we are out until tomorrow.
   c. STOPS SIZED TO THE MARKET. Every invalidation level must sit at least trend.min_stop points beyond the entry zone, and a range call's edges at least trend.min_half_range from spot on each side. In the spread, the SOLD strike sits at or beyond the invalidation level; keep the spread 100 points wide on Nifty and 500 on Bank Nifty so the maximum rupee loss stays where it has been.
